## 3. Beta software, and what that means for your files

Peach Commander is a file manager in beta. It copies, moves, renames and deletes files, and it can execute plugins and macros that do the same. It is under active development and it can contain defects.

- Keep backups of anything you cannot afford to lose, and verify a destructive operation before confirming it.
- The application is currently **not signed or notarized** by an Apple Developer ID. macOS therefore blocks the first launch until you explicitly allow it. This is documented on the installation page; it is a consequence of the project not holding a Developer ID, not of anything the app does.
- Use at your own risk, within the limits of the Apache License.
